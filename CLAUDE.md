# phd-029 - HPT and Extremism

Manuscript *"Cross-Cultural Validation and Ideological Fairness of a
Historical Perspective Taking Instrument: Evidence from Czech Secondary
Students"*: does a Historical Perspective Taking (HPT) instrument conflate
right-authoritarian agreement with disciplinary reasoning? N = 293
students, 10 schools; preregistered on OSF (https://osf.io/zsngy/).
Result: no ideology effects; historical knowledge is the only consistent
predictor. Desk-rejected five times (TRSE, EJPE, AME, IJT, SPOE); sole
active route PsyArXiv -> PCI Psychology -> Peer Community Journal.

Public reproducibility package: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23494026>.
The former OSF Project DOI is retained only as provenance.

**Hub:** `README.md` (status, Next steps, Log). Read it first; at the end
of a session update Next steps and Log.
**Details:** `docs/study-and-pipeline.md` (sample, constructs,
hypotheses, files, Rmd pipeline 01-07, Zenodo package, conventions).

## Project rules

- Preregistered study, **not** a Registered Report: never write
  "Stage 1/2" or "Registered Report".
- Do not name the upper-secondary school types (no "gymnázia").
- Files with `CONFIDENTAL`/`CONFIDENTIAL` in the name and teacher reports
  are never committed or shared.
- POP items are reverse-scored (`5 - POP`); z-scored predictors end in
  `_z`; random intercepts for school and class.
- Manuscript: APA 7, American English; figures 1-4 come from scripts
  `fig02`-`fig05`.
